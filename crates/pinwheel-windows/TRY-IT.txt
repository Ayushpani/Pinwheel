Pinwheel for Windows: early test build
=======================================

This build is not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Pinwheel needs no install and no internet.

1. Double-click Pinwheel.exe. A pinwheel icon appears next to the clock
   (it may be hidden under the ^ arrow). A note says "Pinwheel is ready".
2. In File Explorer, start dragging an image, several images or a folder.
3. While still holding the mouse button, press Shift. The wheel appears.
4. Move onto a slice and let go:
     JPG, PNG, WebP, ICO, TIFF   convert to that format
     50%                         half the width and height
     1920 px                     longest side 1920 px
     500 KB                      JPG under 500 KB
5. A notification shows what happened. Click it to see the result.

Results are saved next to the originals. Originals are never changed.
Right-click the tray icon to save results in a "Pinwheel" subfolder or a
folder you choose, to make a ZIP when converting several files, or to
start Pinwheel with Windows.

What to check, and tell us about
- Does the wheel appear when you press Shift during a drag?
- Does it appear when it should not (for example Shift while selecting text)?
- Does dropping on each slice give the right result, next to the original?
- Dragging a whole folder: are all images converted, and subfolders kept?
- Does moving off the wheel, or pressing Esc, close it and let the drag carry on?
- Is anything slow, blurry, cut off at the screen edge, or wrong on a second monitor?

Known gaps in this build
- Images only (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO). No HEIC, PDF, audio or video yet.
- Photo dates and location are not copied to the results yet.
- No hover size preview, keyboard control or screen-reader names on the wheel yet.
